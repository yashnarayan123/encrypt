Why the LAND flip dies here but lived in Task 3: the tag is
verified BEFORE parsing, so garbage never reaches the lenient
parser. CBC had no tag -- its only hope was the parser, and the
parser was (deliberately) forgiving.

Pattern leak, distinct 16-byte blocks (Task-3 numbers remeasured
live just now for the side-by-side):
  plain=68 ecb128=69 cbc128=3073 gcm128=3072 (of 3072 blocks)
  GCM looks like CBC (noise), not ECB (silhouette): no padding in
  CTR plus a fresh keystream per nonce randomises every block.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
BONUS -- nonce reuse under one key (misuse warning, not a break):
  CT1 XOR CT2 == PT1 XOR PT2: True
  attacker knowing PT1 recovers PT2 exactly: True
  Same-key/same-nonce also leaks the GHASH key H over time, which
  is why secure_send() refuses nonce reuse (guard tested in
  tests/test_link.py). GCM is only as good as its nonces.
